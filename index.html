Understanding the origins of the universe, life, and mind.

Sciences of the Origin brings together philosophy and the sciences to investigate how we can understand the deep past through evidence, inference, interdisciplinary research, and critical reflection.

Explore the project

The Sciences of the Origin

Questions about origins occupy a unique position at the boundaries of science and philosophy. They concern events and processes that cannot simply be observed directly, and therefore require the reconstruction of the past from incomplete and often indirect evidence.

The Universe

Cosmology and the philosophical questions surrounding the reconstruction of cosmic history and the origins of the universe.

Life

The origins and early evolution of life, and the methodological challenges involved in reconstructing processes in the deep biological past.

Mind

The emergence of consciousness, cognition, and human capacities, including the evidence available for understanding our deep evolutionary history.
